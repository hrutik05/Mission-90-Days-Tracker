Preparation rules

1. Accuracy before speed.
Build clean methods first, then reduce time.
2. Error notebook is compulsory.
Record concept errors, calculation errors, traps and time leaks.
3. Current affairs every day.
Do not postpone GA to the final month; revise monthly.
4. Mocks need analysis.
A mock without review does not tell you what to fix.
5. Local-language readiness.
Check the language requirement for the State/UT you choose when the notification arrives.
